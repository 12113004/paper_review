Abstract: This paper presents a self-compensating StrongARM latch topology tailored for high-speed and energy-efficient true random number generator (TRNG) design. Unlike conventional approaches that rely on auxiliary controllers to monitor the output and apply compensation, the proposed latch incorporates a coupling capacitor and internal feedback path, which autonomously mitigate offset voltages induced by transistor mismatch. This inherent self-compensation not only simplifies the overall architecture but also improves robustness against PVT variations.
