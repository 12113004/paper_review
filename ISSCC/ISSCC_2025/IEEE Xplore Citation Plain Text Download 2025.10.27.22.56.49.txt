Therefore, analog filtering along the receiver path is often needed to attenuate blockers and preserve the desired signal quality. While low-order analog filters can mitigate far-out blockers, nearby blockers require high-order bandpass or notch filtering. Prior high-order analog filter designs [1]–[11] have been demonstrated but are often limited in reconfigurability in terms of changing filter response or passband/notch frequencies.
