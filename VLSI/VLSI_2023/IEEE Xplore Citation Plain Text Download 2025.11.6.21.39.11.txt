The TX delays are shared for different firing angles in plane-wave beam-forming, reducing the number of stored delay values by 99.8%. Exchanging the processing order for the proposed coarse delay finding scheme reduces the storage size by 78.1% for elastography imaging. The chip dissipates 169mW at 200MHz from a 0.91V supply.
